# Year 9 Timber Technology – End of Year Examination

**Total Marks: 80**  
*Reading time: 5 minutes – Writing time: 90 minutes*

---

## Instructions to Students
- Attempt **all** questions.
- Answer the multiple-choice questions on the provided answer sheet.
- For short-response and extended-response items, use full sentences and labelled diagrams where appropriate.
- Use industry terminology and reference relevant Australian Standards or WHS regulations when applicable.

---

## Section A – Multiple Choice (30 marks)
Each question is worth 1 mark. Select the **best** answer for each scenario.

1. While preparing to cut a length of Tasmanian oak, Luca notices the blade guard on the drop saw sits slightly higher than normal after the last class used it. What is Luca’s **first** action?
   - A. Continue cutting carefully while observing the blade guard.
   - B. Report the issue to the teacher and tag the saw out of service until inspected.
   - C. Lower the guard manually while the blade is spinning.
   - D. Switch to hand tools to finish the cut.

2. Aria is laminating two pieces of pine using PVA glue. The clamps are leaving faint dents in the surface. What should she do to maintain the finish quality?
   - A. Tighten the clamps further to ensure a strong bond.
   - B. Place scrap timber pads between the clamp jaws and the workpiece.
   - C. Remove the clamps and rely on glue pressure alone.
   - D. Apply a quick coat of sealer before gluing to harden the surface.

3. During a group project, Sam suggests skipping the material moisture content test because the timber “feels dry.” What is the most appropriate response from the team leader?
   - A. Agree and proceed; experience is usually enough to judge dryness.
   - B. Insist on testing because inaccurate assumptions can cause warping.
   - C. Delay the project until a specialist can visit.
   - D. Soak the timber overnight to stabilise it.

4. Mei is sanding a tabletop with 80-grit paper and wants to achieve a furniture-grade finish. What is the correct progression?
   - A. Move directly to 240-grit for a faster result.
   - B. Increase to 120-grit, then 180-grit, finishing with 240-grit.
   - C. Switch to steel wool immediately.
   - D. Apply stain before continuing to sand.

5. Jayden is selecting a finish for an outdoor planter box made from treated pine. Which option best suits durability and environmental exposure?
   - A. Interior polyurethane varnish.
   - B. Water-based wall paint.
   - C. Exterior-grade penetrating oil.
   - D. Clear shellac polish.

6. A cordless drill battery has swollen slightly and no longer seats properly in the charger. What is the safest course of action?
   - A. Puncture the battery to relieve pressure before recycling.
   - B. Wrap the battery in tape and keep using it until it fails.
   - C. Inform the teacher and follow the school’s e-waste disposal procedure.
   - D. Try forcing it into the charger to reshape the casing.

7. A mitre joint on a picture frame consistently gaps on the inside edge even after re-cutting. What should be checked first?
   - A. Whether the timber is kiln-dried.
   - B. Accuracy of the saw’s bevel angle calibration.
   - C. If the glue has expired.
   - D. The grit of sandpaper used on the faces.

8. Rani plans to use a router to cut a decorative edge but is unsure of the feed direction. What is the correct guidance?
   - A. Feed the work so the bit pulls it along the fence.
   - B. Feed against the rotation of the bit to maintain control.
   - C. Feed from both directions to balance the cut.
   - D. Feed with the grain only, regardless of rotation.

9. During WHS induction, students discuss managing airborne dust. Which combination is most effective in a school workshop?
   - A. Dust masks only when a teacher is present.
   - B. Dust extraction, PPE (respirators), and regular housekeeping.
   - C. Opening windows and using personal fans.
   - D. Sweeping at the end of term and storing offcuts outside.

10. Ella’s cutting list calls for 12 identical components. The teacher recommends using a stop block on the drop saw. Why?
    - A. It keeps the saw blade sharper for longer.
    - B. It prevents the timber from slipping under the guard.
    - C. It ensures consistent lengths while improving safety.
    - D. It allows two pieces to be cut at the same time.

11. While assembling a cabinet carcass, Noah notices the plywood sheet has a slight bow. What is the best strategy before fastening?
    - A. Nail the bowed section first to draw it flat.
    - B. Flip the sheet so the bow faces outward.
    - C. Clamp the sheet square within the frame before fixing it.
    - D. Plane down the edges to match the bow.

12. A student wants to identify the species of an unknown hardwood board. Which observation provides the most reliable evidence?
    - A. Colour of the aged surface only.
    - B. Density, grain pattern, and end-grain pores together.
    - C. Smell after cutting with a saw.
    - D. Presence of sapwood on the edges.

13. When using a belt sander on a large panel, why should the user keep the sander moving?
    - A. To prevent the dust bag from overfilling.
    - B. To avoid gouging or creating low spots in the timber.
    - C. To reduce vibration damage to the motor.
    - D. To save electricity by finishing faster.

14. In a risk assessment, which control is **most effective** for preventing accidental contact with a table saw blade?
    - A. Posting warning signs near the saw.
    - B. Wearing thick gloves while cutting.
    - C. Installing a functioning blade guard and riving knife.
    - D. Limiting usage to small offcuts.

15. Zoe’s design folio needs justification for selecting plantation-grown timber. Which rationale is most appropriate?
    - A. Plantation timber eliminates all transport emissions.
    - B. It supports sustainable supply with predictable quality control.
    - C. Plantation timber is always cheaper than recycled timber.
    - D. It requires no finishing due to controlled growth conditions.

16. A coping saw blade keeps twisting while cutting an intricate curve. What adjustment should be made?
    - A. Reduce tension to allow the blade to flex freely.
    - B. Increase blade tension and ensure the blade teeth face the handle.
    - C. Rotate the frame to cut backwards.
    - D. Apply wax to the blade to prevent twisting.

17. Ava is comparing nails and screws for fixing a back panel to a pine frame. Which factor best supports choosing screws?
    - A. Screws require fewer pilot holes.
    - B. Screws provide superior withdrawal resistance for maintenance access.
    - C. Nails are more likely to split the pine.
    - D. Screws are less affected by moisture.

18. A lathe tool rest is set 30 mm away from the turning blank. Why is this unsafe?
    - A. It prevents proper sanding while the lathe spins.
    - B. The gap allows the chisel to catch and lever violently.
    - C. The tool rest blocks the operator’s view.
    - D. The lathe motor must work harder with a gap.

19. During a design evaluation, which criterion best measures the **functionality** of a timber desk organiser?
    - A. Amount of recycled material used.
    - B. Accuracy of mitre joints.
    - C. Capacity to hold specified stationery items securely.
    - D. Visual appeal of the finish.

20. Which statement about seasoning timber is **most accurate**?
    - A. Air seasoning is faster than kiln seasoning.
    - B. Kiln seasoning allows controlled conditions to minimise defects.
    - C. Seasoning is optional if timber is stored indoors.
    - D. Seasoned timber always weighs more than green timber.

21. Marley is cutting dovetails by hand and keeps breaking tails near the baseline. What technique adjustment is needed?
    - A. Saw slightly away from the waste and pare back with a chisel.
    - B. Saw past the baseline to ensure a tight fit.
    - C. Use a coping saw to remove the entire joint.
    - D. Switch to a softer timber without dovetail practice.

22. A classmate suggests using MDF for a bathroom vanity shelf. What should you point out?
    - A. MDF cannot be cut with standard carbide blades.
    - B. MDF swells in humid environments unless sealed properly.
    - C. MDF is heavier than stone benchtops.
    - D. MDF will not accept any paint finishes.

23. During router setup, the collet nut feels gritty and resists tightening. What is the appropriate action?
    - A. Lubricate the collet with machine oil and continue.
    - B. Clean the collet and bit shank, then reassemble securely.
    - C. Tighten with additional leverage until the grit clears.
    - D. Wrap the bit in tape before inserting.

24. When preparing a risk control plan, which option best represents an administrative control?
    - A. Installing emergency stop switches on machines.
    - B. Scheduling student access so only trained users operate machinery.
    - C. Purchasing safer, low-toxicity finishes.
    - D. Wearing hearing protection.

25. A set square indicates a joint is out of square by 3°. Which tool is most suitable for correcting the joint quickly?
    - A. Block plane to trim mating surfaces.
    - B. Marking knife to deepen the scribe line.
    - C. Cabinet scraper to remove finish.
    - D. Centre punch to shift the joint.

26. Sienna is applying water-based stain in warm weather and sees it drying unevenly. What technique should she use?
    - A. Apply in smaller sections and maintain a wet edge.
    - B. Add turpentine to slow drying.
    - C. Sand between coats with 40-grit paper.
    - D. Heat the workshop so the stain levels out.

27. Why is it important to reference Australian Standard AS/NZS 4063 when designing structural timber components?
    - A. It lists the tax rates for timber imports.
    - B. It specifies strength grading requirements for structural timber.
    - C. It describes furniture design trends.
    - D. It provides packaging guidelines for timber shipments.

28. During project planning, a student identifies a critical path in their Gantt chart. What does this path represent?
    - A. The most expensive tasks in the project.
    - B. Tasks that can be delayed without affecting completion.
    - C. The sequence of tasks that determines the overall project duration.
    - D. A list of tasks assigned to the teacher.

29. When re-sawing timber on the band saw, what setup helps minimise drift?
    - A. Using a narrow, fine-tooth blade with low tension.
    - B. Aligning the fence with the natural drift angle of the blade.
    - C. Removing the fence and cutting freehand.
    - D. Tilting the table slightly toward the operator.

30. A sustainability audit reveals a large amount of short offcuts. Which strategy best addresses this issue in future projects?
    - A. Burning offcuts at the end of term to clear space.
    - B. Ordering longer boards to ensure material availability.
    - C. Incorporating modular dimensions and reuse plans into designs.
    - D. Purchasing only pre-cut components from suppliers.
